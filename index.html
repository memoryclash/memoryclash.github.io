How to play

The basics

  1. Pick a mode: play against AI bots, pass the device around, or create an online room and share the invite link.
  2. On your turn, flip any two face-down cards. Everyone sees them, so watch carefully and remember where things are.
  3. Match: if both cards are the same, they stay face-up in your color, you score 1 point and you go again.
  4. Mismatch: the cards stay visible for about a second, then flip back and the turn passes to the next player.
  5. Winning: when every pair is found, the most points wins. A tie is broken by flip accuracy (matches ÷ attempts).

Special rules

  • Streak bonus: every 3 matches in a row earns +1 bonus point. A mismatch resets your streak (🔥 shows your streak).
  • Hint: each player gets one hint per game. At the start of your turn, tap 💡 Hint to peek at two random hidden cards for about 2 seconds.
  • Turn timer: if time runs out, your turn passes. The host can set 10s, 15s, 30s or no timer.
  • Best of five: wins are tallied across games in the same room. The first player to 3 wins takes the match.
  • Bot level: Easy bots remember 2 cards, Normal 4, Hard 8. More memory means more matches.
  • Online rooms: up to 4 seats. Only the host can change settings, add bots or start the game. If someone disconnects, the game skips them, and if the host leaves, another player takes over.
  • Chat & emoji: in online rooms, tap 💬 to message the room or send a quick emoji reaction.
  • Refreshed or locked your phone? Reopen the page and you'll slide back into your seat automatically.
